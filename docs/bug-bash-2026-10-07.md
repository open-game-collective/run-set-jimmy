### BB-001: no turn guidance on the phone
**Expected:** the phone whose turn it is says so and says what to do.
**Actual:** only the action buttons changed; players drew, took discards and discarded without realising it was their turn (this confusion produced BB-003 and BB-004).
**Fix:** a turn banner (`src/client/turn-line.ts`, unit-tested) — "Your turn: draw a card", "…take the 8♦ now, or wait for the deck", "…Cat wants the 8♦", "…go down if you can, then discard", "Ben's turn · you can buy the 8♦" — plus a haptic tick and chime when the turn arrives. Repro test failed before, passes after.
