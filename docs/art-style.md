# Run Set Jimmy: art style

**A supper-club card room, 1962.** Not a casino and not a kids' game: a grown-up table under a
low brass lamp.

- **Felt:** deep teal-green (`--felt #0f3b37`, darker `--felt-deep #0a2725`) with a soft lamp pool in
  the middle of the TV and a faint woven grain.
- **Brass:** trim, turn glow, the buy-window ring, buttons (`--brass #c9a24a`, `--brass-hi #ecd08a`).
- **Card stock:** warm cream (`--paper #f6efe0`), ink suits (`--ink #1d1b19`) and oxblood suits
  (`--oxblood #9b2335`). Faces (J, Q, K) are a big italic Fraunces letter in a ruled frame: no
  portraits, no faces on anything. The Joker is a brass starburst with "JOKER" set vertically.
- **Card backs:** art-deco fan pattern, oxblood on brass.
- **Type:** Fraunces (display: titles, ranks, scores), Bricolage Grotesque (UI text).
- **Motion:** cards slide and settle; the discard flips; a brass sweep runs along a meld when
  someone goes down; going out gets a short fan of cards. Nothing bounces.
- **No emoji, no faces on objects, nothing over the table (the TV's focal area).** The top-right
  corner of the TV stays empty for the OGS launcher's invite card.
